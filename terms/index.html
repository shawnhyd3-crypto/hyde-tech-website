Working with Hyde Tech Solutions

Hyde Tech Solutions provides IT consulting, network support and AI automation services. Contact info@hydetech.ca to discuss your project.

Scope and quotes

Website descriptions introduce the services available. A written quote or service agreement sets out the work, pricing, applicable taxes, third-party charges and payment arrangements for your project.

Booking and changes

An enquiry does not reserve a time or create a service agreement. Availability, scheduling, cancellations and any refund arrangements are confirmed before you commit to paid work. This website does not accept payments.

Technology and examples

Illustrations and sample conversations explain possible setups; they are not customer results. Equipment compatibility and AI capabilities depend on the systems involved and need testing for your use case.

Access to your systems

Only request work on systems you own or are authorised to manage. The access and responsibilities needed for the work should be agreed before it begins. Do not send passwords through an initial enquiry.

Questions

Ask Shawn about the scope or terms of a project before accepting a quote. Nothing on this page limits rights that cannot be excluded under applicable law.
