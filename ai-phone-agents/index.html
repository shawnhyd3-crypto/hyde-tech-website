Phone agent questions

Can I keep my business number?

Call forwarding can connect your existing number to an AI agent. We’ll check your current phone provider and setup before recommending an approach.

Can the AI book appointments?

Appointment booking depends on the plan and the calendar or scheduling software you use. We’ll confirm compatibility and test the booking flow before it goes live.

What happens when a caller needs a person?

We can define a follow-up process and, where supported by your setup and plan, a transfer to a person. We’ll agree on how exceptions should be handled.

Are calls recorded?

Recording and transcription options depend on your setup. The recording notice, consent process, access and retention need to be agreed for each setup. You can also contact Shawn by email.

Do you work outside Toronto?

Hyde Tech serves the Greater Toronto Area and surrounding areas. Tell me where you’re located and what needs to be done so we can confirm on-site or remote options.
