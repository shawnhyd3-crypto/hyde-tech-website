Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 21, 2026

Hyde Tech Solutions ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. It applies to visitors of hydetech.ca, people who contact us, and users who interact with advertising technology operated by Hyde Tech Solutions on behalf of our clients.

1. Who we are

Hyde Tech Solutions is an IT consulting and business automation firm based in Ontario, Canada. We provide consulting, AI phone agents, workflow automation, network design, security camera systems, and digital advertising services to small and mid-sized businesses.

2. Information we collect

Information you provide

When you contact us through our website, email, or phone, we collect the information you choose to share — typically your name, email address, phone number, business name, and the content of your message.

Information collected automatically

When you visit our website, our hosting provider and analytics tools automatically collect standard technical information such as your IP address, browser type, pages viewed, referring site, and timestamps.

Advertising and analytics data

We use the Meta Pixel, Google Analytics, and similar advertising and measurement tools on our website and — with their authorization — on client websites we manage. These tools place cookies or similar identifiers in your browser and may share information with Meta (Facebook and Instagram), Google, and comparable third parties for analytics and advertising measurement. Data collected by these tools may include pages viewed, buttons clicked, device and browser information, and a pseudonymous identifier tied to your browser.

3. How we use information

  • To respond to inquiries and deliver the services you request
  • To send service-related communications (estimates, invoices, project updates)
  • To improve our website, services, and client campaigns
  • To measure and optimize advertising we run for ourselves and our clients
  • To comply with legal obligations and enforce our agreements

4. Advertising services and client relationships

Hyde Tech Solutions operates advertising and measurement technology on behalf of client businesses — for example, running Meta and Google advertising campaigns that direct people to a client's website. When you visit a client website that uses our Meta Pixel or similar tools, any data collected is processed on behalf of that client and may be shared with platforms such as Meta for advertising optimization. Each client remains responsible for its own privacy practices; consult the client's own privacy policy for details specific to that site.

5. Cookies and tracking

We use cookies and similar technologies for functionality, analytics, and advertising. You can control cookies through your browser settings, and you can opt out of interest-based advertising from participating companies through tools such as the Digital Advertising Alliance of Canada or Meta's Ad Preferences.

6. Sharing your information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only with:

  • Service providers that help us operate our business (hosting, email, payments, CRM, analytics) under contractual confidentiality obligations
  • Advertising platforms (Meta, Google, and similar) in connection with campaign delivery and measurement
  • Clients, when you contact us about work we are doing on their behalf
  • Legal and regulatory authorities if required by law

7. Data retention

We keep personal information only as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes it was collected, to comply with legal obligations, and to resolve disputes. Inquiry emails are typically retained for up to 24 months unless a business relationship is established.

8. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect information against loss, unauthorized access, and misuse. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, but we work to keep your information safe.

9. Your choices and rights

Subject to applicable law (including Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act), you may:

  • Request access to the personal information we hold about you
  • Request that we correct or delete your personal information
  • Withdraw consent for marketing communications at any time
  • Opt out of advertising cookies through the links in section 5

To exercise any of these rights, email info@hydetech.ca.

10. Children

Our services are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

11. International users

We operate from Canada. If you access our website from outside Canada, your information may be transferred to, stored, and processed in Canada, where data-protection laws may differ from those in your country.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the most recent revision. Material changes will be announced on this page.
