Privacy & cookies

Hyde Tech Solutions is Shawn Hyde’s IT consulting and automation business serving the Greater Toronto Area. Contact info@hydetech.ca with privacy questions or requests.

Visiting this website

This website does not use advertising pixels, analytics scripts or tracking cookies. Fonts and images are served with the site. The interactive examples run in your browser and do not make phone calls.

GitHub Pages hosts the site and records visitor IP addresses for security. See GitHub’s privacy statement for its handling of technical information.

Contacting Shawn

Email links open your email application. Nothing is sent until you send the email. Include only the information needed to discuss your enquiry; please do not send passwords or sensitive account details.

Your enquiry and contact details are used to respond and discuss the work you request. Contacting us does not sign you up for marketing. Email services process messages as part of delivery and storage, and may process information outside Canada.

Information and requests

Contact Shawn to ask about information held about you, request access or correction, or discuss deletion and withdrawal of consent. Records needed for ongoing work or legal obligations may need to be retained. Privacy questions and concerns can be sent to info@hydetech.ca.

Client systems

AI agents, recordings and other services configured for clients have their own processing requirements. Providers, access, consent and retention should be agreed as part of that service before it starts.
